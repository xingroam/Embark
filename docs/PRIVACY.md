# Privacy Policy

## Overview

Embark is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data and what rights you have.

## Security and Privacy Protection

We take the following measures to protect your privacy:

- Local Operation: All features run locally without needing a network connection.
- Minimal Data Collection: Only essential MD5-processed device identifiers are collected.
- Minimal Permissions: We only request essential system permissions.
- Data Encryption: All sensitive data is encrypted using industry-standard encryption methods.
- Access Control: Strict access controls are implemented to protect your data.

## Data Collection Policy

Embark follows a minimal data collection policy:

- No collection of personal information.
- No collection of device identifiers for licensing or billing purposes.
- No tracking of usage data or analytics.
- No collection of system information.
- No tracking or monitoring of any kind.

All data is stored on your device and never leaves your system.

## Required Permissions

Embark requires macOS Accessibility permissions to function correctly. This permission is necessary for the app to interact with other applications and manage windows.

## Your Rights

As an Embark user, you have the following rights:

- Full control over your data. All settings are stored on your device.
- Access to your personal data and information about how it is processed.
- The ability to delete the software and all related data at any time.
- Freedom from any marketing messages or push notifications.
- The right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority if you believe your rights have been violated.

## Third-Party Services

We use third-party services to provide certain functionality.

## Data Retention

Our data retention policy is as follows:

- Local Data: All app data is stored locally on your device and is retained until you delete the application.

## International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure that such transfers comply with applicable data protection laws.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons.

We will notify you of any material changes by posting the updated Privacy Policy and revising this document.
